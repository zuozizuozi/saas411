# Pricing specification

- Centered title/subtitle, monthly/yearly segmented control, three plans and an FAQ.
- Annual pricing is the emphasized default and displays the discount badge.
- Middle plan is marked most popular with a stronger blue-violet border.
- Cards use the same navy surface, 12px radius and compact feature list as the product shell.
- Checkout configuration remains Stripe-driven; the UI does not invent product IDs.
