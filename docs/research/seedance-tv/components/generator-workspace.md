# Generator workspace specification

- Desktop boarding region begins directly under the 52-64px product header.
- Two-column canvas: 430-470px settings card and flexible examples/results area.
- Settings card uses `#0f172a`, `#263244` borders, 8-12px radii and 14px body text.
- Heading is 20px; section labels are sentence case and 14px semibold.
- Prompt limit is 20,000 characters and includes a prompt-enhancement switch plus deterministic helper action.
- Text-to-video omits all source-image controls.
- Image-to-video exposes two equal upload wells labeled First frame and Last frame. The first frame is required; last frame is optional.
- Shared settings: model, aspect ratio, duration, resolution, audio, watermark removal and output count.
- Aspect ratios are compact radio choices: 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:3, 3:4, 21:9 when supported.
- Bottom summary shows estimated credit cost and one full-width generation button.
- Mobile stacks everything into one card and keeps every touch target at least 40px.
