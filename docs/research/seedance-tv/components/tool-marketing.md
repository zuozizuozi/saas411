# Tool marketing specification

- After the interactive boarding area: centered SEO heading, showcase video grid, workflow/feature blocks, how-to steps, guide cards, FAQ, final CTA and footer.
- Desktop section headings follow 36-48px scale; mobile follows 24-30px scale.
- Cards use restrained navy surfaces and 1px borders. Motion is limited to video playback and subtle hover transitions.
- Copy and brand assets are original VideoFly content; no competitor logo or trademark presentation is copied.
- Sample media is configured centrally and treated as replaceable launch content.
